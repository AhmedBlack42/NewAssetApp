# Phase 5 Clothing 12-Class Mapping

Approved ordered classes: `bra,dress,footwear,lingerie,outerwear,pants,panty,shirt,shorts,skirt,socks,top`. `top` is retained because it is the actual dataset class; the stale `sweater` target was removed. IDs are zero-based in this order. The old Asset CNN 26-class map and checkpoint remain historical provenance and are not remapped. The new 12-class checkpoint has not been trained or created.
