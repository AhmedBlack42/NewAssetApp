# Phase 5 Clothing Final Tag Workflow

The review dialog separates the original CNN broad-family proposal, a human-reviewed 12-class family, and a detailed selectable Clothing tag. Family-based suggestions remain suggestions; a person chooses the final tag. Saved payloads retain the original CNN class, human family, human tag, input-method marker and taxonomy version. Fine-grained dataset eligibility requires a family-matched tag and confirmed crop.
